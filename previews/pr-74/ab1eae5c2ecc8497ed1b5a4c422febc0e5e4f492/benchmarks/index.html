BEFORE COMPARING THE BARS

Same question.
Same work.

A useful result tells you what ran, where it ran, and what the timer included.

  1. 01

    Match the workload

    Use the same dataset, reader count, update size, and retained history. Compare native collections and incremental copies too.

  2. 02

    Keep the environment attached

    Browser exports include your browser identity, hardware concurrency, source commit, and dependency versions. Recorded reports retain their original runtime and host.

  3. 03

    Open the method and raw samples

    The live suites rotate case order, warm up, and check results. Export the JSON after the run. Startup and module loading are outside the timers; memory has a separate method.
