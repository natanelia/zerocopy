## Subject and scope

The initial screen covers Node 22.23.3 and Bun 1.4.2 on Ubuntu x64 in separate clean jobs. The 20 cases in `trie-view-workloads.mjs` include ordinary/natural-sorted empty and singleton numeric map entries (four), ordinary/sorted empty and singleton set values (four), HAMT collision-two and patched-32 entries (two), ordinary/sorted 4096-entry first-next-return (two), warmed existing-key get (two), canonical/patched HAMT and canonical/four-edit-journal radix full entries (four), and ordinary/sorted object-map keys (two). Public operations and fixed fixture shapes are checked outside timing. Each process has exactly one operation and one build.

The prior combined lazy-scratch candidate `5c8b066` remains rejected: Node x64 patched-32 latency +5.18%, interval +4.39%..+5.98%, all eight pairs adverse. The narrowed empty/singleton-only candidate `04227cf` remains held: Bun ARM collision-two +2.40%, interval +1.18%..+3.63%, all quartets adverse. Their original study artifacts and conclusions are not modified or reinterpreted by this screen. The current candidate introduces neither scratch-allocation nor empty-return changes.

The view-capture mechanism preserves executed allocation expressions, not necessarily generator-frame memory. Capturing an extra local can change engine frame size or retention. The mechanism proof does not measure heap allocation, retained bytes, or runtime speed. Public fixture payload hashes and allocator positions establish immutable WASM bytes and no shared allocation during reads; they make no claim about JS heap allocation.

## Frozen work and interpretation

All workloads, source/build/compiler pins, schedules, protocol constants and this workflow are frozen before pilot execution. There are two disposable fresh-process pilots per case, one per build. Pilot warmup runs for at least 500 ms and 1024 operations under explicit 15-second / 100-million-operation caps. Post-warmup calibration has at most 16 steps and exactly three samples per step, targeting 40 ms. The last reported repeat was actually sampled. Pilot caps invalidate the plan; they do not silently truncate or change it.

For every case, common fixed repeats are ceil(40 ms / fastest observed post-warmup milliseconds per operation across both builds). Common warmup is ceil(500 ms / fastest rate), rounded up to complete common-repeat batches. This provides prospective 4x batch-floor and approximately 3.33x warmup-floor headroom. The fastest rate can come from either build or any calibration step. All 20 common plans and the complete seeded schedule are frozen before the first measured process. A pilot failure prevents the measured phase and retains every partial output. Maximum repeat is 10 million; maximum warmup work is 100 million operations. No clamp or later rate adjustment is allowed.

Every measured process executes exactly the prescribed warmup work followed by 21 fixed-work batches. No elapsed-driven extensions, adaptive retries, discarded batches, or explicit GC. Every batch must be at least 10 ms, and actual warmup must be at least 150 ms. Any floor, cap, subject, integrity or completeness failure invalidates that case's inference, while retaining its data. A runtime failure ends that job with complete/partial receipts; a timing-floor flag is retained while the remaining scheduled subjects continue unchanged. Job retries are refused by requiring GitHub run attempt 1.

Each case has four A/B quartets and four independent baseline A/A quartets, with exactly two ABBA and two BAAB orientations for each mode. Modes are interleaved by a fixed seed. The 21-batch median gives each process's per-operation time. Two adjacent left/right process pairs provide two log ratios; their mean is one quartet replicate. The four independent quartet log ratios determine an exponentiated mean and pointwise two-sided 95% Student-t interval with df=3. Batches and adjacent pairs are never treated as independent replicates. There are 640 measured subjects and 13,440 batches per runtime, plus 40 pilots.

The predefined material-loss margin is 2%: upper interval <=1.02 is evidence within margin; lower interval >1.02 is detected material loss; otherwise inconclusive. A/A role drift requires its geometric point estimate outside [1/1.02,1.02] and its interval excluding 1. Drift invalidates that case. Report A/A and absolute times directly; never normalize A/B by A/A. An inconclusive control or target cannot be called a pass or offset by target gains. The screen reports all cases within margin only when every A/B cell is valid and within margin. A separate label indicates whether a target's interval establishes gain (upper <1). Neither label establishes portable acceptance.

Intervals are pointwise and unadjusted across 20 cells and two runtimes. Four quartets give limited precision, and a hosted runner can produce inconclusive intervals. No post-result extra samples are authorized. ARM64 and separate Chromium/Firefox/WebKit correctness and performance work require review after this screen. This x64 gate cannot settle the historical Bun ARM risk or support browser-performance claims.

## Isolation and evidence

Each subject launches at the exact same physical neutral root, with original `package.json` bytes and complete `dist/` contents copied anew. Both builds use `dist/shared.js`, identical helper paths, cwd and executable, and default JIT settings/randomness. No role/build label is sent to the subject; labels exist in the controller receipt only. Compile cache is disabled. Symlinks, mismatched package bytes, altered helper files, source drift, compiler drift, different root WASM bytes or any of the exact twelve emitted JS bundle files cause failure. Every subject records its before/after full file manifest, physical entry/package/subject paths, command, raw stdout/stderr, exit/signal/error, and parsed data. Failed output is archived before validation.

The job first builds and checks both pinned sources: full unit suite, ordinary types, post-declaration strict public worker-consumer types, both typed-value modes, Redux/geometry types, declaration build, installed-package checks, both Node/Bun shared/copy actual workers, and the candidate differential mechanism proof. Deterministic protocol/source/workload/negative tests use `.node.mjs` names so Vitest does not rediscover Node's test runner. The baseline worktree is outside the project in the runner temporary directory. All 40 required prerequisite commands must exit zero before timing. The seal and freeze validators require exactly that full check set, including both Bun and Node full suites and every original worker helper. Check commands, raw logs, exit/signal/error, outcome and hashes are retained in `prerequisites.json`. Every original tracked proof-code helper and root test is guarded against its immutable ref.

The freeze archives both complete dist trees, original package bytes, guarded source inputs and all twelve original WASM files, proof source, compiler receipts, workflow at its original hidden path and a visible copy, schedules and prerequisite hashes. Upload is `if: always()` with `include-hidden-files: true`. Complete or partial records, frozen plans, and every child output remain available when later work fails. Caught subject failures include in-memory partial sample arrays in stdout; forced termination such as timeout/OOM can lose those arrays, leaving the retained command, started receipt, raw streams and exit/signal/error as the available evidence. Such a record cannot validate as completed. Verification requires the declared runtime result and binds every pilot and measured subject to its exact chronological schedule slot. Fresh builds must reproduce the independently collected baseline and candidate pins before any pilot.

## Entrypoints

- `node --test proofs/trie-view-*.node.mjs`: deterministic/untimed validation only.
- `node proofs/trie-view-gate.mjs prepare BASE_ROOT CANDIDATE_ROOT OUTPUT`: freeze verified sources, prerequisites, bundles and protocol; no timing.
- `node|bun proofs/trie-view-gate.mjs run OUTPUT`: requires the reviewed first-attempt GitHub job, pinned runtime and frozen receipts; performs pilots and measurements once.
- `node|bun proofs/trie-view-gate.mjs verify OUTPUT`: recheck frozen evidence and complete records.

The workflow is `.github/workflows/trie-view-capture.yml`, scoped to the preparation branch and manual dispatch. Parent review is required before publishing the branch or opening a PR.
