## Bounded execution and retained failures

Every whole command has a fixed 300000 ms deadline, separate from the unchanged per-test timeout. A Linux process group owns its child processes. The runner sends SIGKILL at the deadline or on wrapper interruption, waits for the direct child, and checks `/proc` for live group members before finalizing. Zombies/dead entries cannot execute or append; live survivors or an unverifiable cleanup fail closed and prevent the next command. A separately declared 1000 ms cleanup bound is recorded. Deliberately detached descendants that escape the group are outside this process-group guarantee.

Start headers appear immediately in CI and in command logs. Raw stdout/stderr go straight to evidence files during execution. Atomic manifest replacement preserves a prior valid receipt if interrupted; failed exits, signals, timeouts, partial logs and cleanup results are retained. Neither duplicate commands nor existing log replacement is allowed. Normal validation failures do not stop the declared counterpart checks, so a baseline failure cannot suppress the candidate observation. Setup/integrity failures, whole-command timeout, interruption or unresolved cleanup stop dependent execution. Synthetic runner tests can create nested process groups; stopping after any timeout/interruption prevents a later check from running beside an escaped synthetic child, but host cleanup may still be needed for that child. All recorded failures remain failures; a missing command, changed command/order, raw-log mismatch or any nonzero/timeout/interruption/cleanup failure prevents sealing.

The setup step is capped at 10 minutes, validation at 30 minutes and the job at 45 minutes. Always-run build retention, portable archive and artifact upload follow. The archive keeps original filenames, hidden paths, raw bytes, proof-root dependency resolution locks and partial receipts. Sudden host loss or uncatchable termination may still prevent a final receipt/upload; an unfinished receipt cannot pass. The workflow cannot turn a later successful focused test into a replacement full-suite pass.
