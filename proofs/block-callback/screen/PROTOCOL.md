## Uncertainty and decisions

A process contributes the median of its 21 batches divided by the common scan count. Within each six-process block, take the mean log median for each source's two processes. Each C/main or C/PR23 observation is the difference of those two source means. The eight block contrasts are the independent units for a pointwise two-sided 95% Student-t interval: exp(mean ± 2.3646242515927844 × sample SD / sqrt(8)), df 7. Batches and duplicate subjects inside a block are not independent replicates. Report every C/main and C/PR23 result, all three AA intervals, raw block contrasts, and absolute process medians. No multiple-comparison/global guarantee is claimed.

Time ratios are candidate/comparator. A lower bound above 1.02 is detected material loss; upper bound at or below 1.02 is evidence within the scoped margin; otherwise the row remains inconclusive. AA invalidation occurs when the later/earlier AA point is outside [1/1.02, 1.02] and its interval excludes 1. Full AA interval containment is a separate equivalence label. No AA normalization is performed.

Admission to broader validation requires complete usable evidence, no detected material loss on any measured target or control against either comparator, and at least one large target's C/PR23 upper bound strictly below 1/1.02. Inconclusive controls remain visible and do not automatically prevent admission. A main-only improvement is insufficient. A favorable screen permits broader investigation only, not adoption, PR acceptance, or a universal performance claim.

Any warmup cap, calibration cap, batch below 10 ms, warmup below 150 ms, prescribed-work mismatch, AA drift, missing process, output discrepancy, or lifecycle failure makes the relevant inference invalid/incomplete and prevents admission. Preserve valid completed observations even when the campaign stops. Do not drop losing cases, substitute samples, pool with old studies, rerun for favorable results, extend sampling, or combine with PR23 view capture.
