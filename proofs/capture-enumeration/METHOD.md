This descriptive observation includes the full first session constructor and initial capture after collection setup. It is not a cold-runtime measurement and is separate from warmed publication. Parent spawn-to-readiness-receipt includes imports, primary fixture construction, scheduling and readiness-message overhead, and excludes the intended constructor operation; delivery may overlap child progress, so it is a descriptive process-startup observation, not API latency. ## Sampling and fixed bounds

Use six blocks per engine, each containing independent fresh baseline B, candidate C and second-baseline A processes: **36 measurement children**. A imports exactly B's baseline artifacts. Block orders are, once each: `B C A`, `A B C`, `C A B`, `B A C`, `C B A`, `A C B`. Alternate engine-first order: Node first in odd blocks. Run children sequentially on one hosted machine without concurrent build/test/benchmark work. Record hardware, OS, host/job identity, runtime versions, flags and timestamps.

One baseline-only calibration child per engine selects counts, giving **38 children maximum**. For every row start at 256 operations; each trial has five warm-up batches and three pilot batches. Use the pilot median to target 30 ms, accepting 20–50 ms. Otherwise scale by `30 ms / median`, round to the nearest positive integer and clip to the cap. At most four trials; no candidate calibration. Caps are 2^20 operations/batch, except Flush may use 2^24. Uncalibrated W24-changed blocks measurement; an uncalibrated control retains its last attempted count and is measured as unresolved. Retain calibration data but exclude them from estimates. Freeze counts before measurement; no further tuning.

At its frozen count, each measurement row receives **five discarded warm-up batches and nine measured batches**. Retain every raw elapsed duration, count, checksum, validation outcome and constructor/readiness observation. No trimming, outlier deletion, selective repeats or optional stopping. A process observation is the arithmetic mean of its nine elapsed-per-operation values. Mark quality unresolved for any nonpositive/nonfinite duration, measured batch below 10 ms, within-process CV above 25%, or last-three/first-three batch-mean ratio outside 0.85–1.15. Keep all flagged data. Any primary quality failure blocks a gain verdict; control quality failures remain explicit. Wrong output, missing process, timeout, crash or resource termination makes the whole screen incomplete.

The entire performance study, including calibration, fixtures, launch, collection, analysis and cleanup, has a **14 min 30 s hard wall cap**, **90 s per child**, and no replacement children. Sample summed process-group RSS every **50 ms**; terminate at **2 GiB**. This is a sampled safety guard with possible overshoot/shared-page double counting, not a physical-memory guarantee or outcome. Use the same sampler for every child; its scheduling cost is a limitation. No address-space limit or runner reconfiguration. Terminate/reap original owned groups on caps/interruption and preserve partial evidence. Remove only study-created disposable fixtures/build copies after recording their digests.

## Analysis and prospective decision

For each row/engine/block calculate `d = log(C/B)`. From the six block values report `exp(mean(d))` and the paired two-sided 95% Student-t interval `exp(mean(d) ± 2.570582*sd(d)/sqrt(6))`. Processes, not batches, are independent replicates. No bootstrap/resampling, cross-engine pooling, choice between baselines or replacement of B with a favorable average. Small-n inference assumes approximately independent, reasonably behaved block log-ratios; balance/diagnostics do not prove that on a shared host.

Report matching intervals for `log(A/B)` and `log(later baseline / earlier baseline)`, plus chronological observations. Major systematic bias means the entire interval lies above 1.05 or below 0.95. Major W24-changed or Flush baseline bias blocks a gain verdict; other biased controls are unresolved. An isolated point or interval crossing one is not a regression; report scatter even when inconclusive.

A meaningful primary lead requires W24-changed's ratio **≤0.95 and 95% upper bound ≤0.95 in both engines**, with complete source/correctness/quality admission. Any control with **95% lower bound >1.02** in either engine parks the candidate. A control upper bound above 1.02 without a lower bound above 1.02 is unresolved, not passed; no additional samples follow. Constructor/startup observations remain descriptive unresolved diagnostics, and apparent material adverse results require review before promotion. The joint engine requirement is a conjunction; unadjusted control intervals are conservative warnings, not simultaneous no-regression certification.

Report absolute ns/op, all process means/paired ratios, intervals, calibration/quality/baseline diagnostics, constructor/startup observations and limitations. A passing result is only a promising hosted publication-latency lead with named unresolved controls. Failure to meet the primary criterion ends this screen without seeking tighter bounds. Preserve commands/cwd, source/artifact/runtime pins, environment, stdout/stderr/exits, all raw JSON, manifest and analysis inputs/output.
